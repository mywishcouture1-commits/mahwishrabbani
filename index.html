Meet the Owner

Personal style, personal service

For more than twenty years, Mahwish Rabbani has hand-selected bridal, party and casual clothing from Pakistan for the women of Long Island. Her store, My Wish, is family owned and family run.

When you visit, you're welcomed by Mahwish and her family, who will help you find the right outfit, the right size and the right jewelry to complete the look.
