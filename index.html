Meet the Owner

Personal style, personal service

For more than twenty years, Mahwish Rabbani has hand-selected bridal, party and casual clothing from Pakistan for the women of Long Island. Her store, My Wish, is family owned and family run.

When you visit, you're welcomed by Mahwish and her family, who will help you find the right outfit, the right size and the right jewelry to complete the look.

Dressed for every occasion

From the mehndi and mayun to the nikah, baraat and walima, Mahwish helps brides and their families find the right bridal lehenga, sharara or gharara. For Eid ul Fitr, Eid ul Adha and Chand Raat, choose from festive shalwar kameez, anarkalis and embroidered lawn suits. Guests will find party wear for engagements, dholki nights and every celebration, finished with kundan sets, jhumkas, tikkas and bangles.

My Wish carries Pakistani clothing for women and girls, branded designer suits and ready-to-wear, with personal help on sizing in store.

Everything you see here, and much more, is available in store at 420 S Broadway, Hicksville.
